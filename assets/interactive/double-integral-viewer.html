Double integrals

The integral ∫∫ f(x,y) dx dy is the volume under the surface z = f(x,y). Doing the x-integral first gives the area A(y) of one slice; integrating those areas over y sweeps out the volume. Drag the slider to move the slice.

y = 0.00

The edges can depend on y: try 0 and y for a triangle, or -sqrt(1-y^2) and sqrt(1-y^2) for a disc. Areas and volumes here are computed numerically (Simpson's rule in x, trapezoid in y), so you can check your hand-worked answers against them.
