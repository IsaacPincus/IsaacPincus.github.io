Parametrised curves

A curve in space is a map t ↦ φ(t) = (x(t), y(t), z(t)) from an interval onto ℝ³. Drag the slider to move along the curve. The green arrow is the position vector φ(t); the red arrow is the velocity φ′(t), drawn from the point and at true scale.

t = 0.00

Type any expression MATLAB would accept: sin, cos, exp, sqrt, abs, pi, and ^ for powers. The velocity is found numerically, so you don't need to supply derivatives.
